# Yukima 雪間 — tokens

A cool blue-grey identity with an earth-and-growth ramp, built on 60-30-10. Separate from [`ana-blue`](../ana-blue/palette.md) and [`reiser-warm`](../reiser-warm/palette.md). Every value below is either present in `yukima.pptx` or explicitly marked **derived**.

Full ratio table for all three palettes: [`../../references/contrast-matrix.md`](../../references/contrast-matrix.md), generated by [`../../scripts/contrast.py`](../../scripts/contrast.py).

## 60% — the field

Six surfaces carry the page and never carry text.

| Token | Hex | Role | ANA Blue equivalent |
|---|---|---|---|
| 寒の戻り *kan-no-modori* | `#F1F6FA` | The default page, set on the master. "The cold's return." | `#FFFFFF` |
| Off-white | `#F5F3F1` | Lifted surface — callout and takeaway bands. | `#E6F2FC` |
| 雪解け *yukidoke* | `#C8DAE8` | Inset surface — cards, panels, the Quote field. "Snowmelt." | `#F0F6FC` |
| Light blue | `#AACEE7` | Rules, borders, table gridlines, dividers. | `#C3D6EE` |
| Sage | `#CAD2C4` | Inactive and deprecated states. | `#AFC0D6` |
| 三寒四温 *sankan-shion* | `#E1EDD5` | Pale category tint. "Three cold days, four warm." | — |

Separation across the family is **1.02–1.52:1** — deliberately low, the same one-family discipline the other two templates hold. For a hard boundary use an `#AACEE7` rule, not a fill change.

## 30% — the supporting layer

**Both inks are derived.** The source palette's darkest colour is `#4B6F87` at 4.92:1 — enough for body copy, far short of a heading. These two are darkenings of that exact hue (H 197.8°, S 0.317), so the page stays one blue family:

| Token | Hex | On field | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Headline ink **derived** | `#22333D` | **12.00** | Titles, body, panel body, callout body. |
| Muted ink **derived** | `#3B576A` | **7.00** | Captions, footers, source lines, axis labels, the Two-Column "Before" band. |
| Dark blue *as type* | `#4B6F87` | **4.92** | Eyebrows, panel headings, big numbers, links. See the dual-role note. |
| Reverse | `#FFFFFF` | 5.35 on `#4B6F87` | Text on the accent fill and the divider field. |

## 10% — the accent

| Token | Hex | On field | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dark blue *as area* | `#4B6F87` | — | Title rules, spines, badges, table header fills, chart series 1, the divider field. |
| 常磐木 *tokiwagi* | `#558860` | 3.80 | Second accent fill — "evergreen". **Display sizes only as text.** |
| Text-safe 常磐木 **derived** | `#4D7B57` | **4.50** | Use when the green must be type. |
| Mid blue | `#7499BA` | 2.75 | Accent rule and chart series. **Fill only.** |

One accent as area per page.

**The dual role of the dark blue.** As with `ana-blue`, the accent is text-safe, so `#4B6F87` occupies the 30% when it is type and the 10% when it is area. The ceiling governs area, not type. `reiser-warm` has no such ambiguity because coral cannot be text at all.

## Three traps

**Nothing in the source palette carries white text comfortably.** Bands take the accent or the muted ink, never a green or a gold:

| Fill | White on it | Ink `#22333D` on it | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| `#3B576A` muted ink | **7.62** | 1.42 | white — the darkest band available |
| `#4B6F87` accent | **5.35** | 2.44 | white |
| `#558860` 常磐木 | 4.14 | 3.16 | white, ≥ 24px only |
| `#BD7E1A` ochre | 3.41 | 3.83 | either, ≥ 24px only |
| `#86B655` 萌黄 | 2.38 | **5.49** | **ink** |
| `#D2AD52` gold | 2.13 | **6.12** | **ink** |

**萌黄 and gold want dark text.** Same mid-tone trap as `reiser-warm`'s coral, and the same fix.

**Nine of the fifteen source colours are fill-only** on the field: `#7499BA` `#AACEE7` `#C8DAE8` `#F5F3F1` `#CAD2C4` `#E1EDD5` `#86B655` `#9A9641` `#D2AD52`. Four more are display-size-only: `#558860` `#7C754F` `#BD7E1A` `#5F7890`. That leaves `#4B6F87` as the only source colour legal as body text — which is why the two inks had to be derived.

## Extended categorical

The source board organises these as an "earthy, organic contrast series" and a "warm complementary palette". They sit outside the 60-30-10 bands — a chart is data, not page furniture. As fills they are all fine; as text on the field none clear 4.5:1, so each carries a derived text-safe variant.

| Name | Fill | On field | Text-safe | Variant ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 常磐木 *tokiwagi* | `#558860` | 3.80 | `#4D7B57` | 4.50 |
| 萌黄 *moegi* | `#86B655` | 2.18 | `#587A35` | 4.54 |
| Olive | `#9A9641` | 2.83 | `#777332` | 4.51 |
| 憩い *ikoi* | `#7C754F` | 4.28 | `#78714D` | 4.52 |
| Ochre | `#BD7E1A` | 3.13 | `#9A6615` | 4.50 |
| Gold | `#D2AD52` | 1.96 | `#8A6D24` | 4.50 |
| Mid blue | `#7499BA` | 2.75 | `#4C7499` | 4.53 |
| Slate | `#5F7890` | 4.22 | `#5B738B` | 4.52 |

Chart series order: `#4B6F87` → `#558860` → `#86B655` → `#9A9641` → `#BD7E1A` → `#D2AD52`. Cool to warm, which is the palette's own logic — snow through growth to earth.

Project Status spine order: `#4B6F87` → `#558860` → `#86B655` → `#9A9641`.

**There is no approved red or amber for risk.** Ochre reads as warmth here, not alarm. If a deliverable needs a risk colour, ask rather than repurposing one of these.

| 28pt | Quote body | `#22333D` |
| 24pt | Agenda numeral | `#4B6F87` |
| 21pt | Cover subtitle, takeaway, rationale | `#22333D` |
